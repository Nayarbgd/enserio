DESIGN NOTES:
- Overall aesthetic: ultra-dark luxury gym, cinematic and premium, like a high-end 
Dubai brand.
- All section headlines are bold, condensed, uppercase, large (60-80px).
- Gold section labels above headlines are spaced uppercase, small (14px), amber color.
- Background is always near-black with subtle texture or noise, never pure white.
- Buttons: primary = amber fill + black text, secondary = transparent + white border.
- Images: all black & white or dark-tinted to match the brand.
- WhatsApp floating button in bottom-right corner (green circle with icon).
- Mobile-responsive layout with hamburger menu on mobile.
- Smooth scroll animations: fade-up on section reveal, no excessive effects.
- Font pairing: Barlow Condensed or similar for headlines, Inter or similar for body.
